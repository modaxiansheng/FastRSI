FROM DECISION TO EXPERIENCE ↓

01 THE IDEA

An interaction is more
than an action.

It is a decision to validate, an opportunity to deliberate, and an experience to learn from. FastRSI connects these moments in a recursive framework for embodied agents.

I

Remember what holds.

Retain decisions together with the conditions under which they remain valid.

II

Reason where it matters.

Use memory to guide when additional deliberation is needed.

III

Turn experience into learning.

Connect execution feedback to the design of a continuing improvement loop.

02 THE SYSTEM

Three modules.
One recursive idea.

Explore the research design, from reusable decisions to feedback from execution.

VALIDITY BEFORE REUSE

Decision Validity
Memory

A memory of decisions is useful only when those decisions still apply. DVM associates past decisions with their validity conditions, providing a basis for reuse as the agent interacts with its environment.

Decision→Validity→Reuse
DELIBERATION WHEN NEEDED

Memory-Guided
Sparse Deliberation

MSD uses memory to guide where fresh reasoning is needed. Instead of treating every interaction as an isolated decision, the design distinguishes reusable decisions from situations that call for renewed deliberation.

Memory→Check→Deliberate
FEEDBACK INTO EXPERIENCE

Closed-Loop
Experience Distillation

CED closes the research loop by connecting execution feedback with experience distillation. The goal is to turn interactions into knowledge that can inform future decisions and the agent’s continuing improvement.

Execute→Feedback→Distill

iThese modules describe the research design. The released evaluation uses a fixed, recovery-enabled configuration and does not demonstrate the complete learned loop.
